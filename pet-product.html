From a Vet-Visit Problem to an App: Product Planning

Six steps of rapid product validation: find the problem, check the market, map features, prioritize, plan the roadmap, design the product.

Starting pointUnable to describe a pet's daily condition at the vet
MethodMarket data → feature trade-offs → vibe coding
ProgressMarket analysis done, product in development
Next stepPet owner and vet interviews

1 · Find the problem: the vet-visit journey

The pain clusters in two moments: recalling recent days, and being asked for details by the vet. Those two moments are what the app has to solve.

Orange marks the main pain points. The five steps come from my own vet visits with my pets.

2 · Check the market: is this worth solving?

Dogs and cats at home3.2 millionIn 2025, up about 1.09 million in four years.
Medical share of spending16%Owners' second-largest annual expense, after food.
Competitor gapNeutral record layerNone of five major players keeps care records across stores and clinics.

Full data and competitor comparison: market and competitor analysis.

3 · Map features: mind map

Starting from "clear answers at the vet", ideas branch in four directions.

Branches come from the planned features and the two databases. Home medical care means subcutaneous fluids, blood glucose checks and insulin given at home, which older pets need most.

4 · Prioritize: impact × effort

High impact, low effort (top left) goes into the MVP. High impact but harder (top right) waits for phase two.

Home medical care sits in the MVP: it matters most at the vet, since older pets have the most follow-ups. To build, it is a time-plus-value log like the food log, so effort is moderate and it sits near the line.

5 · Plan the roadmap

Features ship in batches. Each phase has one metric to validate, and the next phase starts only when it is met.

No dates are set: when the next phase starts depends on the previous phase's metric.

6 · Design the product: sitemap and app screens

Five bottom tabs: the four mind-map directions plus Home. Below are five key screens.
