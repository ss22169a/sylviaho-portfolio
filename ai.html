Connecting market data

Windsor.aiGoogle SheetsGoogle AdsAmazon Seller CentralAmazon Ads
Problem
Google Ads sends traffic to Amazon but can't track purchases directly, so each weekly report needed manual exports from several sources lined up by date.
Method
Windsor.ai connectors write Google Ads, Amazon Seller Central (Sales & Traffic, All Orders) and Amazon Ads into one Google Sheet (weekly overview, Google vs Amazon comparison, KPIs, product stats, cancellations and returns), later connecting to Looker Studio, GA4 and Search Console.
Rules
Weeks run Monday to Sunday; Amazon orders lag 1–2 days, so they are marked pending and backfilled; Google–Amazon cross-checks are treated as time correlation, not attribution; every figure records its source, query and extraction date.
Division of work
Amazon Attribution and Brand Analytics stay manual exports; Seller Central access is read-only, and the AI does not edit product pages.
Result
First-week data showed only about 6% of Google clicks became product-page sessions (mainly because ads landed on the storefront). After pointing them to product pages, the click-to-product-page rate rose to 22%.

Analyzing competitors & pricing

Amazon Product Opportunity ExplorerLLM AgentGoogle Sheets
Problem
Comparing our product list with competitor prices and specs one by one was slow, making it hard to spot loss-making or ineffective items quickly.
Method
I export Amazon Product Opportunity Explorer data (alternators, starters and NOx sensors) by hand. An LLM agent working with Google Sheets groups it automatically and cross-checks it against order history, flagging delisted parts, discontinued models and price gaps with same-part competitors.
Key findings and decisions
  • NOx sensors: All 10 SKUs had same-part competitors, and our prices were roughly 100–250% higher than theirs. The core issue was price competitiveness, not visibility, so ads for this line were paused first.
  • Focusing resources: Alternators made up about 93% of revenue, so ad groups and budget now go first to alternators and starters.
Division of work
Data export and strategy decisions stay with a person; grouping, cross-checking and anomaly flagging go to the LLM agent.
Website quality · Product pages and press releases

Automated SEO review

LLM AgentCustom web toolMarkdown reports
Problem
Manual page-by-page review, or SEO tools alone, find errors but can't say what to fix first; scores change from run to run, so there's no comparing with last week; and the reports are hard to hand to the web agency. With four languages across the site, the work doubles again.
Review method
Each run checks one page. Items with a clear right answer (such as page titles and image descriptions) are checked by fixed rules, so results are stable and comparable week to week. Items that need judgment (such as whether specs are complete or wording matches how customers search) go to AI, which may only use facts already on the page. Each issue gets an urgency level and an owner, and items for the agency come with a draft request email. Technical issues (such as page speed) are left to specialist tools.
Pilot results
On Oct 13, 2026, a pilot on 2 pages found 13 issues, 7 of them in shared templates: duplicate meta descriptions, a missing H1, one URL shared by four languages, and a cookie notice naming another company. Fixing single pages wasn't enough, so I added a roll-up that groups the same issue across pages under its template, so it gets fixed once.
Division of work
I paste in the page source, decide which fixes to adopt and how far to take them, and make the CMS updates myself.
Next
Record how long each audit and manual fix takes to get the average time to fix one issue; resubmit sitemap.xml and connect Search Console to track how long fixes take to show up in rankings.

Ad operations with guardrails

Google Ads ScriptsWindsor.aiGoogle Sheets
Problem
With too many metrics and dashboards to watch, anomalies were easy to miss at first, and keyword changes were hard to tie fully to their change log and the reasons behind them.
Method
Scripts run at manager-account level with a Google Sheet as the control panel. Module 1 compares yesterday with 7- and 14-day averages and emails alerts when spend, clicks or CTR cross a threshold. Module 2 classifies search terms by intent and uses n-gram analysis to find wasted root words. Both are read-only. Budget suggestions and AI-drafted ad copy are next.
Guardrails
Changes are capped at ±20% per step; suggestions expire after 7 days; every change logs the original value and who approved it; data is never mixed across accounts; new ads are created paused. Each suggestion must state the observation, cause, action, expected range, and the evidence against it.
Executed with approval
Negative keywords, 9 vehicle-specific ad groups, responsive search ads, CPC caps, pausing an underperforming product line, and location settings.

News-trend writing

LLM AgentGoogle TrendsSEMrushLinkedInCMS
Problem
Datasheets are full of engineering parameters and test conditions, while LinkedIn readers come from many backgrounds. Posting raw specs easily turns into engineering talk or loses key context, and breaking down each piece by hand takes a lot of effort.
Method
I set up the press-release review process, built an AI knowledge base, and wrote rules for company terminology, source weighting and review levels. When writing or reading industry material, I choose the topic, set the angle and write the public content. AI acts as reviewer and analyst: following these rules, it breaks down spec parameters and test conditions, checks that technical terms are consistent, weighs cited sources, and flags issues along with the review level that needs a person.
Example
A MEMS hydrogen-sensor carousel featuring full startup readiness within 70 ms (5.5× faster than the 390 ms market benchmark); real-time detection within 2 s across 0–40,000 ppm; power consumption below 200 mW across operating temperatures from −35 °C to +105 °C; and a compact 2.0 mm footprint achieved through semiconductor silicon micromachining (1/100 the size of conventional 15 mm designs).
Division of work
AI review suggestions and risk flags help with proofreading. I check every technical figure, test condition and term against the source documents before anything is published.

Media relations · AI search visibility

External press coverage

Press releaseMedia relations
Why
When a high-intent buyer asks an LLM agent which industrial hydrogen sensors are on the market, the answer mostly cites third-party sources the platform weights highly, such as news outlets and industry press. A company's own website carries little weight. A new product that no credible outlet has covered rarely shows up in AI answers.
How
I wrote the release to GEO standards so AI can find and cite it: every sentence has a full subject and names the company, the product and the model number HN01001, so no sentence depends on the one before it. I sent it to the media contact for placement in a major outlet, to gain a high-authority third-party source.
Result after publication
On publication day (Nov 20, 2026), a Google search for 氫能偵測器 (hydrogen detector) returned an AI Overview that named the HN01001 as an example and cited this Economic Daily News article, which was also the first source listed beside the answer.
Next
Check from time to time with the questions we want to be found for, to see whether the release did its job.

Design workflow · Product catalog planning

Digital publication design

LLM AgentFigmaProduct catalog
Problem
The engineering specs were too dense for trade-show visitors to take in within a few seconds.
Method
First I set what a visitor should remember within seconds of seeing the catalog and which numbers buyers care about. AI then pulled the copy, six key specs and applications from the catalog in that direction. After building design guidelines to the CIS standard, I laid it out in Figma on a fixed grid that other language versions can reuse.
Division of work
Every figure is checked against the catalog, and each claim keeps its test condition next to it. The layout and final wording are approved by a person before print.

Rapid product validation

Vibe codingSwiftUIFirebaseGovernment open data API
Problem
New product ideas often turn out to be unwanted only after a lot of time and budget has gone in.
Method
I first use AI for market and competitor analysis, then build a working product fast with vibe coding and test it in the market. Real data and user feedback decide whether to invest more money and people.
Division of work
Product direction, feature trade-offs and whether to invest more are my decisions; AI helps with data gathering, competitor comparison and writing code.
Current progress
Applied to a pet care app: the market analysis is done and the product is in development. Next step: interviews with pet owners and vets.
