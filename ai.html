Connected market data

Windsor.aiGoogle SheetsGoogle AdsAmazon Seller CentralAmazon Ads
Problem
Google Ads sends traffic to Amazon but cannot see Amazon purchases. Every weekly report meant exporting several reports from Google Ads and Seller Central, then lining them up by date by hand.
How it works
Windsor.ai connectors read Google Ads, Amazon Seller Central (Sales & Traffic and All Orders reports) and Amazon Ads, and write into a single Google Sheet: weekly overview, Google-to-Amazon comparison, KPIs, product stats, cancellations and returns. GA4 and Search Console are next, through Looker Studio.
Rules
Weeks always run Monday to Sunday. Amazon order data lags one to two days, so recent days are marked pending and backfilled. Google-to-Amazon comparisons are treated as time correlation, not attribution. Every number records its source, query and extraction date.
Human in the loop
Amazon Attribution and Brand Analytics are still exported by hand. Seller Central access is used for reading only — the AI never edits listings.
Result
The first week of joined data showed only about 6% of Google clicks became Amazon product-page sessions, because ads landed on the storefront instead of product pages. Fixing that landing path lifted click-to-product-page rate to 22%.

Competitive & pricing intelligence

Amazon Product Opportunity ExplorerClaudeGoogle Sheets
Problem
Checking our catalog against the market meant searching Amazon product by product and comparing prices one listing at a time.
How it works
Product Opportunity Explorer exports for alternators, starters and NOx sensors are grouped and matched against our own order history. The analysis flags parts we have sold but no longer list, discontinued models, and same-part competitor prices.
Finding
All 10 NOx sensor SKUs had same-part competitors priced 72–273% lower. The problem was price, not visibility, so ads for that line were paused. Alternators made up about 93% of revenue, so budget and ad groups now prioritize alternators and starters.
Human in the loop
I download the source data and decide the strategy; the agent does the grouping and comparison.

Automated SEO audits

ClaudeCustom web toolMarkdown reports
Scope
Product pages and press releases in four languages (English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean), with reports in English.
How it works
31 checks in five modules. 16 fixed rules run in the browser so results stay consistent; 15 checks need judgment, so Claude reviews meaning and drafts rewrites using only facts already on the page. Every issue is tagged: fix it myself, check the CMS first, or hand to the web vendor — and vendor items become a drafted request email. Scores are logged so trends are visible.
Pilot
On Oct 13, 2026 the audit found 13 issues across 2 pages. 7 were template-level — a meta description copying the title, a missing H1, four languages sharing one URL, and a cookie notice naming another company — which showed that fixing pages one by one would not work.
Human in the loop
I paste the page source, decide which fixes to adopt, and make the CMS changes.
Next
Time each audit against the manual process, and connect Search Console to track whether fixes move rankings.

Google Ads automation · Manager account

Guardrailed ad operations

Google Ads ScriptsWindsor.aiGoogle Sheets
Problem
Daily monitoring meant logging in and judging by eye whether numbers looked off, and every keyword or bid change was made by hand without a record.
How it works
Scripts run at manager-account level with a Google Sheet as the control panel. Module 1 compares yesterday with 7- and 14-day averages and emails alerts when spend, clicks or CTR cross a threshold. Module 2 classifies search terms by intent and uses n-gram analysis to find wasted root words. Both are read-only. Budget suggestions and AI-drafted ad copy are next.
Guardrails
Changes are capped at ±20% per step; suggestions expire after 7 days; every change logs the original value and who approved it; data is never mixed across accounts; new ads are created paused. Each suggestion must state the observation, cause, action, expected range, and the evidence against it.
Executed with approval
Negative keywords, 9 vehicle-specific ad groups, responsive search ads, CPC caps, pausing an underperforming product line, and location settings.
