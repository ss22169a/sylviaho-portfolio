Launching the First PUF + FIDO Security Key on Amazon North America: How to Position It?

PUFido, which SecuX describes as the world's first FIDO2 security key built on PUF (physically unclonable function) technology, is entering a category led by well-known brands. How should it find its position?

RoleMarketing Project Manager
Period~4–5 months · 2025
ToolsGoogle Trends · SEMrush · survey · GA4 · Hotjar
ResultTop 5 in its Amazon category within 30 days*

Conclusion

Demand was not the barrier. People feared losing passwords and having accounts taken over, and they were curious about passwordless login, but most didn't understand how a security key works. So the launch led with education, split into two tracks: plain "plug in, tap, done" for everyday users, and standards and hardware security for professionals. The key reached the Top 5 in its Amazon category within 30 days.*

What that meant for a launch

  • The standard was ready. 48% of the world's top 100 websites already supported passkeys in 2025, so a key would work on the accounts people use.
  • The pain was real. 48% of surveyed consumers had abandoned an online purchase because they forgot a password.
  • Awareness ≠ understanding. Knowing the word "passkey" is not the same as trusting a USB key. That gap is what the launch had to close.

Source: FIDO Alliance, Consumer Password & Passkey Trends (World Passkey Day 2025) and The State of Passkeys 2026.

2 · Three lines of research, one insight

Competitor teardownSpecs vs. storyEstablished keys sold on standards and certifications. Few explained, in plain words, what a key does for an ordinary account.
Keyword forecastingWhich wordsGoogle Trends and SEMrush to size search interest around passwordless login and account security, and choose the words the listing and ads would use.
Pain-point surveyFear + curiosityFear of lost passwords and account takeover, curiosity about passwordless login, and low understanding of how it works.

Insight: the barrier was education, not demand. Every message had to answer "what does this do for me?" before "how secure is it?".

3 · Two-track positioning

Everyday users

"No app. Plug in, tap, done."

  • Works with Google, Facebook, Microsoft
  • No codes to type, no SIM-swap risk
  • Message: simple protection for accounts you already use
Security professionals & crypto users

"FIDO2 / U2F, hardware-rooted, cross-platform."

  • Certified standards, phishing-resistant
  • PUF: the private key is generated on demand, not stored
  • Message: a different way to protect the key itself

Budget was split by market (U.S. and EU) and by track. Brand mentions were sorted into a four-tier priority framework so that anything touching security or trust was answered first.

4 · Result, and what I'd keep better records of

Launch result

The key reached the Top 5 in its Amazon category within 30 days of launch. After launch, Zendesk tickets, Hotjar recordings and GA4 data were reviewed together and fed into the next product spec.

* Based on internal tracking at the time. I left SecuX in July 2025 and no longer have access to the original records, so ad-level figures are not shown here.

What I'd do differently

  • Export a dated snapshot of rank, ads and reviews at each milestone, so results stay provable after leaving a role.
  • Fix a baseline period before launch, so every "+X%" has a clear "compared with what".
  • Track the education gap directly: how many visitors read the how-it-works content before buying.
