Turning Taiwan's Hydrogen Policy into a B2B Email Pipeline

A green-hydrogen materials company earns most of its revenue through annual tenders and the partners who bid on them. Policy and subsidy timing tells you when those buyers will start looking. The email program was built around that calendar.

RoleWeb Ops & Marketing Lead
PeriodAug 2025 – Jul 2026
ToolsPolicy tracker · EDM · company filings · GA4
ResultInquiry conversion 0.2% → 2.5%

The short answer

In a market this early, demand follows the state: budgets, demonstration projects and tenders decide who buys and when. So emails were timed to policy and tender milestones, and written for the specific partners likely to bid. Prospects met at energy trade shows got content tailored to their own filings and recent news. Segmented emails reached a 50–60% open rate, and inquiry conversion rose from 0.2% to 2.5%.

2 · Two pipelines into one list

Tender-driven

Follow the money

  • Track national strategy, subsidy and state-owned enterprise tender notices
  • Map which integrators and partners are likely to bid
  • Send a short read of the tender and where the company's materials fit
Trade-show prospecting

Research before you write

  • Collect active exhibitors and visitors at energy and hydrogen shows
  • Check each company's financial reports and recent news
  • Write to its current projects: capacity plans, new lines, bids it has won

4 · One email, annotated

Reconstructed example
FromTellus Materials
ToBid lead, fuel-cell integrator
SubjectFuel-cell demo tenders: what buyers will ask you for 1
Three specification points to prepare before the next notice.

Hi [Name],

CPC's fuel-cell demonstration tender showed how state buyers will specify these projects: budget, timeline and test evidence up front. More demonstration tenders are on the national hydrogen roadmap. 2

What buyers usually ask for

  1. Durability and test data at operating temperature
  2. Material traceability and documentation
  3. Local supply and lead time

Where our materials fit 3

[One application relevant to this segment, with one data sheet.]

Book a 20-minute bid-prep call 4

Tellus Materials · Unsubscribe

  1. Subject names the buyer's task. It says what the reader has to prepare, not what we sell.
  2. Opens with the signal. The tender is the reason to write now; the email goes out when the notice or award is public.
  3. One application, one link. Each segment sees only the use case that matches its bids.
  4. One call to action. A short bid-prep call, timed to their timeline.

The original emails were not kept after I left; this rebuilds their structure, not their exact wording. Trade-show prospects got the same shape, opening with the company's own filing or news instead of a tender.

5 · Results

Open rate50–60%Segmented, policy-timed emails to tender partners and researched prospects.
Inquiry conversion0.2% → 2.5%Share of email recipients who sent an inquiry, before and after the program.

What I'd add today: keep a dated log of every send and its segment, and track how many inquiries turn into bid partnerships, not just replies.
