Turning Taiwan's Hydrogen Policy into a B2B Email Pipeline

A green-hydrogen materials company earns most of its revenue through annual tenders and the partners who bid on them. Policy and subsidy timing tells you when those buyers will start looking. The email program was built around that calendar.

RoleWeb Ops & Marketing Lead
PeriodAug 2025 – Jul 2026
ToolsPolicy tracker · EDM · company filings · GA4
ResultInquiry conversion 0.2% → 2.5%

The short answer

In a market this early, demand follows the state: budgets, demonstration projects and tenders decide who buys and when. So emails were timed to policy and tender milestones, and written for the specific partners likely to bid. Prospects met at energy trade shows got content tailored to their own filings and recent news. Segmented emails reached a 50–60% open rate, and inquiry conversion rose from 0.2% to 2.5%.

2 · Two pipelines into one list

Tender-driven

Follow the money

  • Track national strategy, subsidy and state-owned enterprise tender notices
  • Map which integrators and partners are likely to bid
  • Send a short read of the tender and where the company's materials fit
Trade-show prospecting

Research before you write

  • Collect active exhibitors and visitors at energy and hydrogen shows
  • Check each company's financial reports and recent news
  • Write to its current projects: capacity plans, new lines, bids it has won
