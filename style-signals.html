Locking In the Next Trending Products Before Trends Take Hold in Taiwan

Four years of search data (Nov 2021 – Nov 2025) for a mid-price handbag brand in Taiwan: which styles are rising, how many months worldwide trends take to reach Taiwan, and what to order, test or drop over the following 12 months.

MarketTaiwan, worldwide as lead signal
DataGoogle Trends, weekly, Nov 2021 – Nov 2025
ModelDuckDB: momentum, onset rule, lead-lag
OutputOpportunity matrix + 12-month plan

Conclusion

Taiwan follows worldwide bag searches by about three months. Bag charms took off worldwide in August 2024 and in Taiwan in November 2024; the two series line up best at a 2-month lag (r = 0.83), bowling bags at 3 months. As of November 2025 none of the brand's big lines is growing: totes are flat (-4%). The growth is in small attributes worth testing: bag charms (+124%), old-money style, bowling bags and large-capacity bags. Bucket, saddle and underarm bags are fading. Suede is the next imported signal: up 58% worldwide, with almost no Chinese-language searches in Taiwan yet.

1 · How a bag trend reaches Taiwan

Bag charm, each line indexed to its own peak
Worldwide, "bag charm"Taiwan, 包包吊飾

Staying above twice the 2022 average for three straight months counts as "taking off". Bowling bags fit best at a three-month lag (r = 0.62); suede does not line up yet (r = 0.31), as Taiwan's Chinese-language searches are still near zero.

Lead signals

Watch worldwide first

  • Worldwide Google and Pinterest searches for a material, color or shape
  • Trend-forecaster outlooks and Pinterest's yearly trend report
  • Runway and celebrity bags in fashion-search rankings
Confirming signals

Then check Taiwan

  • Taiwan searches in Chinese for the same attribute
  • Marketplace listings and reviews for the style
  • The brand's own site search, wishlist and return reasons

Worldwide, May–Oct 2025
Suede bag+58%6× its 2022 level worldwide; Taiwan searches for 麂皮包 are close to zero.
Burgundy bag+29%Worldwide demand rising each autumn since 2024; no measurable Taiwan searches for 酒紅色包包.
East-west bag+42%Fast growth from a very small worldwide base; a shape to sample, not to stock.

Near-zero Taiwan searches can mean two things: the trend has not arrived, or shoppers describe it with other words. Before ordering, check which words Taiwanese shoppers use on marketplaces and social posts.

5 · Test before committing stock

Two-week test per style
GoBoth metAd click-through at least 1.2× the catalog median, and pre-orders covering 60% of the first batch within 14 days. Order the full batch.
HoldOne metChange one thing (color, material or price) and run another two weeks.
DropNeitherStop and move the budget to the next signal on the list.

A style enters testing only when its worldwide search has passed the take-off rule and Taiwan searches are up at least 20% on a year earlier. Thresholds are set relative to the brand's own catalog, so they carry over between seasons.

What I changed after the check

  • Watch for viral moments on big lines. Totes rose 30% while a cheap Trader Joe's tote sat among Lyst's hottest products in early 2026. Search momentum alone missed it; a weekly check of fashion-search rankings would have flagged it.
  • Set a minimum level before calling small attributes. Underarm bags (+37%) and large-capacity bags (+12%) sit below a level of 8, where a few weeks of noise can flip the result.
  • Plan for a longer lag. With 2026 data the best fit for bag charms moves to 5 months (r = 0.61). Three months stays the earliest warning; orders now plan for up to five.
  • Check the words, not just the trend. Suede still does not show up in Chinese searches. The next step is to look at which terms shoppers actually use on Taiwan marketplaces before ordering.

Limits

  • Google Trends is relative and sampled; small Taiwan terms bounce between 0 and 10, so single weeks are noise and only six-month averages are used.
  • The lead-lag estimate rests on two attribute pairs (2 and 3 months). It is a rule of thumb, not a fixed constant.
  • Search shows interest, not sales. The test step exists to turn interest into purchase evidence before stock is bought.
