Which Bag Trends to Back Next

A search-signal model for a mid-price handbag brand in Taiwan: which styles are rising, how far worldwide signals run ahead of Taiwan, and what to order, test or drop over the next 12 months.

MarketTaiwan, worldwide as lead signal
DataGoogle Trends, weekly, Nov 2021 – Nov 2026
ModelDuckDB: momentum, onset rule, lead-lag
OutputOpportunity matrix + 12-month plan

The short answer

Taiwan follows worldwide bag searches with a delay of about 3–5 months. Bag charms took off worldwide in August 2024 and in Taiwan in November 2024; the two series line up best at a 5-month lag (r = 0.80). Right now totes are the safe bet to scale (+30% on the largest base), bag charms, bowling bags and underarm bags are worth small tests, and bucket and saddle bags are fading. Suede and burgundy are the next imported signals: up 128% and 77% worldwide, with almost no Chinese-language searches in Taiwan yet.

1 · How a bag trend reaches Taiwan

Bag charm, each line indexed to its own peak
Worldwide, "bag charm"Taiwan, 包包吊飾

A term "takes off" when it holds at least twice its 2022 average for three straight months. The same test on bowling bags gives a best fit at 5 months (r = 0.62). Suede does not line up yet (r = 0.32), because Taiwan's Chinese-language searches for it are still near zero.

Lead signals

Watch worldwide first

  • Worldwide Google and Pinterest searches for a material, color or shape
  • Trend-forecaster outlooks: Heuritech expects bowling bags to grow 14% and barrel bags 22% over the year to February 2027
  • Runway and celebrity bags in Lyst's quarterly index
Confirming signals

Then check Taiwan

  • Taiwan searches in Chinese for the same attribute
  • Marketplace listings and reviews for the style
  • The brand's own site search, wishlist and return reasons

3 · Imported signals not yet in Taiwan

Worldwide, May–Oct 2026
Suede bag+128%14× its 2022 level worldwide; Taiwan searches for 麂皮包 are close to zero.
Burgundy bag+77%Worldwide demand rising each autumn since 2024; no measurable Taiwan searches for 酒紅色包包.
East-west bag+195%Fast growth from a very small worldwide base; a shape to sample, not to stock.

Near-zero Taiwan searches can mean two things: the trend has not arrived, or shoppers describe it with other words. Before ordering, check which words Taiwanese shoppers use on marketplaces and social posts.

5 · Test before committing stock

Two-week test per style
GoBoth metAd click-through at least 1.2× the catalog median, and pre-orders covering 60% of the first batch within 14 days. Order the full batch.
HoldOne metChange one thing (color, material or price) and run another two weeks.
DropNeitherStop and move the budget to the next signal on the list.

A style enters testing only when its worldwide search has passed the take-off rule and Taiwan searches are up at least 20% on a year earlier. Thresholds are set relative to the brand's own catalog, so they carry over between seasons.

Limits

  • Google Trends is relative and sampled; small Taiwan terms bounce between 0 and 10, so single weeks are noise and only six-month averages are used.
  • The lead-lag estimate rests on two attribute pairs. It is a rule of thumb, not a fixed constant.
  • Search shows interest, not sales. The test step exists to turn interest into purchase evidence before stock is bought.
