About

An analyst's rigor to read the market, a planner's ownership to steer it, and a designer's eye for clear data.

  1. Design → retail

    I started in visual design, where analyzing platform dashboards revealed the data logic behind every visual asset. I then co-founded a retail e-commerce brand in a crowded market, building buzz and scaling it to five brick-and-mortar stores within three years.

  2. Global B2B tech

    Over the past few years, I’ve moved into global B2B tech—taking products like FIDO2 security keys and automotive MEMS sensors to market, and owning the full cycle from upstream brand positioning and product analysis to go-to-market execution.

  3. Today · e-Vehicle Semiconductor

    Today, at e-Vehicle Semiconductor in Hsinchu, my core strength lies in bridging data to action: I analyze U.S. Amazon demand and web traffic, engineer the dashboards and AI workflows to track them, and translate those live metrics into concrete GTM strategies and hands-on execution.
