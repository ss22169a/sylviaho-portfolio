About

Analyst rigor, a marketer's sense of the customer, and a designer's eye for clear data.

I started in visual design, co-founded a fashion brand and scaled it to five retail stores, then spent the last few years taking technical products to global markets — from FIDO2 security keys to automotive MEMS sensors.

Along the way I learned that the best go-to-market decisions are made long before the campaign: in search trends, funnel data, and competitor moves. Today I work at e-vehicle Semiconductor, an automotive IC design company in Hsinchu, where I analyze U.S. Amazon demand and web traffic data to shape product positioning, and build AI agent workflows that keep that data connected.
