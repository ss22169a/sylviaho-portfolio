Does Google Search Advertising Lift Amazon Sales?

A funnel and break-even read of Google Ads traffic to an Amazon store, and the go/no-go rule that came out of it.

RoleProject Manager
PeriodOct 7 – Nov 15, 2026
ToolsGoogle Ads · Windsor.ai · Sheets · Looker Studio
ResultTraffic quality ×4, not yet break-even → go/no-go rule

Conclusion

Rebuilding the campaigns around category and vehicle pages made the traffic four times more likely to reach a product page and cut the cost of each product-page visit by 73%. It did not achieve account profitability: product-page conversion rates declined, and weekly revenue reached only 65% of the break-even threshold. So the next decision is not about budget. It is a rule for when budget is allowed to move.

What changed my mind

The click-through rate to product pages jumped from 5% to 22% and looked like a success, but the new landing pages sent people into a long product catalog where only 8 of the 111 viewed listings sold. More traffic did not lead to more conversions. The incremental return (extra revenue brought in by each dollar of ad spend) also fell from 1.77 to 0.60, far below the 2.9 needed to break even.

2 · The dashboard

How to read it

  • Use the phase filter to flip every chart between S1 and S2.
  • The timeline shows daily product-page sessions against the pre-ad baseline; S2 starts on Oct 21.
  • The simulator lets you move budget and the two conversion rates. Budget scales the expected orders and the orders needed by the same factor, so only conversion moves the verdict.

What it is built with

  • Five Google Sheets data sources with typed and formatted fields
  • Calculated fields: conversion rates, ROAS, break-even attainment, simulated attainment
  • Parameters and slider controls for the what-if model
  • A phase filter, log-scale funnel, baseline line and a brand-matched theme
  • Money shown as an index (1 unit = average daily revenue before ads), so no prices or dollar values leave the company

3 · The decision rule

Applied every two weeks
Go≥ 100%of break-even in two consecutive two-week windows. Only then raise budget, in steps of no more than 20%.
Hold80–99%Keep budget flat; change one thing (landing page, keyword set or bid) and read the next window.
No-go< 80%Cut to the vehicle-specific ad groups and fix conversion first: in-stock top sellers, and landing pages that show fewer, proven part numbers.

The account sits at 65% today, so the rule says no-go on scaling: fix conversion and stock before adding budget.

What I'd do next

  • Move budget toward on-Amazon ads for part-number searches, where buyers already know what they need.
  • Send Google traffic only to pages with in-stock, proven sellers, and tag every ad group with Amazon Attribution.
  • Keep this dashboard as the two-week check before any budget change.
