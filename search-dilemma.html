Search's Innovator's Dilemma: Can Google Answer Without Losing the Click?

AI answers keep users on Google, and they also remove the click that Google's ad business is built on. Using public data from 2025–2026, this analysis shows where clicks are disappearing, finds one place where they are growing, and proposes experiments to test the fix.

QuestionCan AI answers and search ads grow together?
EvidencePew · Seer · SparkToro · Ahrefs · Alphabet filings
MethodCross-study comparison plus a transparent CTR model
OutputFive experiments for Google and for brands

The short answer

The click hasn't disappeared. It has moved. When an AI Overview appears, every kind of click falls. But on the same results page, brands cited inside the answer get 41% more ad clicks than brands that aren't. So the way out of the dilemma is to make citation the unit that is earned, measured and sold, not to defend the ten blue links. Treat that as a hypothesis to test, not a finding.

1 · The dilemma in four numbers

Zero-click searches68%of U.S. Google searches ended without a click in Jan–Apr 2026, up from about 60% in 2024 (SparkToro, Similarweb panel).
AI Overview effect−58%click-through for the #1 organic result on keywords that now trigger an AI Overview (Ahrefs, 300K keywords, Dec 2025).
Search revenue+12%year over year in Q3 2026, down from 17% the quarter before, even as Google reported its fastest query growth in five years.
Google's explanationAI ModeIts queries monetize at a lower rate. Google is now scaling ads inside AI Overviews and AI Mode, including multi-turn ads in follow-up questions.

That is the innovator's dilemma in plain numbers: the better product (a direct answer) cannibalizes the profitable one (a page of links with ads around it). Query volume is growing, but the revenue from each query is not keeping pace.

Three studies, three methods, one direction. Pew measures real browsing; Seer measures 53 brands' organic and paid search data. Pew also found that only 1% of visits included a click on a link inside the AI summary.

3 · The exception: being cited

Seer split the same AI Overview results pages by whether the advertiser's brand was cited in the answer:

Ad click-through, brand not cited11.19%
Ad click-through, brand cited15.74%

Informational queries, every month of 2025. Cited brands also earned about 120% more organic clicks per impression than uncited brands on the same results pages. The answer box doesn't end the user's journey; it chooses whose brand the user trusts next.

Formula: blended CTR = s × 16.21% + (1 − s) × 21.85%, using Seer's February 2026 rates. The third figure assumes the citation lift measured on informational queries carries over to all AI Overview pages. That assumption is untested, and experiment 01 below is designed to test it. AI Overviews already appear on about 86% of question searches and 95% of comparison searches (Seer), so coverage is far above the 20% average for some kinds of searches.

5 · The cost of a wrong answer

An answer carries Google's voice

A ranked link leaves the judgment to the user. An AI summary states its answer as fact. That is why AI Overviews' early mistakes in 2024, such as suggesting glue on pizza, became public-relations stories, not just ranking bugs. The more often Google answers directly, the more each bad source costs it.

And the supply of bad sources is rising

Mass-produced AI pages are exactly what Google's scaled content abuse policy (introduced in March 2024) targets, and enforcement has picked up. Google ran separate spam updates in June, August and September 2026, and its staff now describe scaled content as a bigger problem than link spam. Answer quality depends on the quality of the citations, so the anti-spam work and the revenue problem are one problem.

6 · Five experiments worth running

Each one has a hypothesis, a test and a pass mark
01
For Google

Sell the citation, not the space around it

Test a labeled "sponsored source" inside AI Overviews. The advertiser's page is offered as one of the cited sources, but only if it passes the same quality bar as organic citations, and it is priced per qualified visit.

Hypothesis
Recovers the 41% citation lift on AI Overview pages
Pass mark
Ad CTR on test pages ≥ the 21.85% non-AI baseline, with no drop in answer satisfaction
02
For Google

Price follow-up questions on intent

AI Mode queries are about three times longer than typical searches, so each follow-up says more about what the user wants. Price multi-turn ads on the stage of the conversation (research, compare, buy) instead of per keyword.

Hypothesis
Later turns convert like transactional searches
Pass mark
Revenue per AI Mode session closes half the gap to classic search within 2 quarters
03
For Google

Give sites a citation report

Add AI Overview citations, impressions and clicks to Search Console, broken out by query. Sites that can see what earns citations will write for citation rather than for volume, which shrinks the scaled-content problem from the supply side.

Hypothesis
Visibility shifts publisher effort from volume to quality
Pass mark
Fewer scaled-content flags among sites that use the report
04
For brands

"Cite-and-bid": a matched-pair test

Pick 40 informational queries and split them into matched pairs. In each pair, rewrite one page to be easy to cite: a direct answer, specs in tables, a named source. Bid on both queries the same way for 8 weeks.

Hypothesis
Rewritten pages get cited more often, and ad CTR rises on those queries
Pass mark
Ad CTR on cited queries at least 20% above control
05
For brands

Report share of answer, not rank

Track a fixed set of buyer questions every week across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity. For each question, record whether the brand is cited, where, and next to which competitors. Report this alongside rank and sessions.

Hypothesis
Citation share leads branded search and direct traffic by weeks
Pass mark
A lagged correlation that holds for 2 straight quarters

Experiments 04 and 05 build on work I already do. The SEO audit workflow checks pages for structured, citable answers, and the category analysis showed that buyers search by exact part number and model year, the kind of precise fact an AI answer cites.

Limits, stated plainly

  • The studies use different panels, time windows and definitions, so their figures are compared for direction, not added together.
  • The citation lift is a correlation. Strong brands may simply be cited more often and clicked more often. Experiment 04 is designed to separate the two.
  • The model holds ad impressions and price constant. In practice Google can change both.
  • This is an outside-in analysis of public data, with no internal Google data.
