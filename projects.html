Analysis complete
Question
Where does paid traffic leak on its way to an Amazon order, how much revenue did the ads actually cause, and where should the next dollar go?
Data
A U.S. automotive aftermarket seller's Google Ads (ad group × day) and Amazon Seller Central data (ASIN × day, order lines), modeled in DuckDB SQL. Dollar values withheld; shares and indexes shown.

Built for: Amazon

Amazon Category Intelligence

Analysis complete
Question
A seller lists about 580 auto parts on Amazon but sells a few a month. Is the problem demand, price, stock, or the listings themselves, and what should be fixed first?
Data
Amazon Product Opportunity Explorer (category, matched-product and unmet-demand data), the seller's All Listings report and 22 months of orders, joined by LESTER / OE part number in DuckDB SQL.
Deliverables
A demand-vs-catalog map for alternators, starters and NOx sensors, and six fixes ranked by the demand each one reaches.
