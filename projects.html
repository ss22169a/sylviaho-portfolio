Beyond the Plateau: Taiwan E-commerce

Analysis complete
Question
Is Taiwan's online retail really flattening? If so, where should a brand seller put channel budget as Coupang and cross-border platforms reshuffle the market?
Data
MOEA retail statistics 2019–2025, listed-platform results, Coupang filings, customs-based cross-border data and DMA ad-market figures, with a plateau test in DuckDB.

Online growth fell from 29% to under 3% a year and its share has held at 13–14% since 2021; new growth is going to Coupang, cross-border imports, store-based retailers and retail media.
