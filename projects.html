Locking In the Next Trending Products Before Trends Take Hold in Taiwan

Analysis completeE-commerce
Question
Can a design-led bag brand spot trends early? Over the next year in Taiwan, which styles should it scale, test or drop?
Data
Four years of weekly Google Trends data from Nov 2021 to Nov 2025 (12 Taiwan and 5 worldwide bag attributes), with momentum, take-off and lead-lag analysis run in DuckDB.
Deliverables
Taiwan trends trail worldwide by about three months. The main lines are flat; bag charms, bowling bags and large totes are rising, and suede is the next-wave signal that has not reached Taiwan yet.
