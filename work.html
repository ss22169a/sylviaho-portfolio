e-vehicle Semiconductor · Automotive aftermarket parts · 2026

Can Paid Search Pay Off on Amazon? Funnel & Break-even Analysis

Funnel analysisAutomotiveAmazon US
Funnel Analytics
RoleProject Manager
PeriodOct – Nov 2026
ToolsGoogle Ads, Windsor.ai, Sheets
ResultClick-to-page 5% → 22%
Business question
Can Google Ads drive profitable Amazon US sales for a long-tail automotive parts catalog (alternators, starters, sensors) — and at what budget?
Data & method
Joined daily Google Ads spend and clicks with Amazon product-page sessions and orders; compared against a 4-week pre-ad baseline; analyzed product-level sales concentration; built a break-even model from average order value, margin, and organic order baseline; added Amazon unmet-demand data and competitor price checks.
Key insight
Google Ads cannot see Amazon purchases, so in-platform conversions read zero — the real signal had to come from Amazon sessions and orders. Broad category keywords drew clicks, but only about 5% of them reached a product page.
Decision
Rebuilt campaigns around product- and vehicle-specific keywords that land on product pages with Amazon Attribution tags; set a profitability threshold and two-week observation windows before any budget change; flagged overpriced listings and low-inventory SKUs.
Impact
Click-to-product-page rate 5% → 22%; weekly Amazon revenue +139% and orders +54% vs. the pre-ad baseline (Oct 7 – Nov 15, 2026); October 2026 was the store's best month in two years (+73% YoY).
What I'd do next
Shift budget toward on-Amazon ads for part-number searches, where buyers already know what they need, and keep the break-even model as the go/no-go rule for Google Ads.
Dashboard coming soonSQL + Looker Studio: daily clicks → product-page sessions → orders, with the pre-ad baseline.

Building an Evidence Base for a Challenger in Hydrogen Sensing

Market intelligenceSemiconductorsIn progress
Market Intelligence
RoleProject Manager
PeriodNov 2026 – present
ToolsSheets, datasheets, industry data
StatusValidation pending
Business question
How does a new MEMS hydrogen-sensor entrant earn credibility with OEM and infrastructure buyers before it has third-party validation or brand recognition?
Data & method
Built a market-intelligence tracker covering MEMS and hydrogen-sensing market size (CAGR recalculated and checked against each source's claim), demand-side indicators (FCEV sales, fueling stations, regulations) cross-checked across two or more independent sources, competitor specs from datasheets, competitor go-to-market stages, and market caps.
Rigor

Key insight
The gap is credibility, not technology. Supporting evidence for every external claim has been assembled; third-party validation is the next milestone.
Status
Validation results (certifications, test reports, customer references) will be added here once available.

SecuX · Hardware security · 2025

Launching a First-of-its-Kind Security Key on Amazon US

Launch researchHardwareAmazon US
Go-to-Market
RoleMarketing Project Manager
Period2025
ToolsGoogle Trends, SEMrush, surveys
ResultTop 5 in category, 30 days
Business question
How do we position the world's first unclonable FIDO2 security key against established brands, and where should a limited ad budget go?
Data & method
Competitor technical and product teardown; Google Trends and SEMrush keyword forecasting; a pain-point survey; a post-launch feedback loop through Zendesk, Hotjar, and GA4 that fed the next product spec.
Key insight
Users feared lost passwords and account takeovers and were curious about passwordless login, but didn't understand it — so the barrier was education, not demand.
Decision
Positioned on two tracks: "no app, works with Google and Facebook, simple" for mainstream users; "FIDO2/U2F, cross-platform, hardware security" for security professionals. Allocated U.S./EU budget by market and built a 4-tier reputation-response framework from brand-mention data.
Impact
Top 5 in its Amazon category within 30 days of launch; U.S. paid ads ROAS 2.3 (+60%), CPC −30%, conversion rate +12% (2025).
Dashboard coming soonLooker Studio: ad performance by market (CPC, CTR, conversion) and a competitor positioning matrix.

BLANK SPACE · Co-founder · Fashion e-commerce & retail

Taking a Digital-Native Brand Offline Without Breaking the Funnel

O2O launchConsumer researchRetail
Go-to-Market
RoleCo-Founder & Marketing Lead
PeriodDec 2020 – Jul 2024
ToolsGA4, Hotjar, SEMrush
Result+2,000 members, 15% CVR
Business question
Should an online-only handbag brand open physical retail — and how do we make offline traffic feed online sales instead of cannibalizing them?
Data & method
Market trend tracking, consumer decision-path research, and competitor promotion analysis.
Key insight
Young buyers of higher-priced bags follow a "research online → try offline → buy online" path, and most competitors only ran online discounts — leaving in-store experience as an open gap.
Decision
Designed a pop-up store as an O2O loop: online invitations (LINE, email, ads, partner media) → in-store experience and member sign-up → online repurchase offers.
Impact
+2,000 members (120% of target), 15% conversion, +30% social engagement, NT$2.5M in store sales in the first month. Across the brand: NT$100M+ revenue within two years of launch (company total), CAC −35%, Shopify conversion rate +67%, and five stores in three years.
Dashboard coming soonLooker Studio: O2O funnel — online reach → store visit → sign-up → repurchase.
